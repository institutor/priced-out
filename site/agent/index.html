The Sealed Envelope

You give an AI agent a question about real people. It runs the survey research through Flashpoint by itself. Before each survey goes out, the agent bets on what people will say, and so do you. The bets go in an envelope.

When the answers come back, the envelope is opened. Whatever was guessed wrong is the thing nobody understood yet, so that is what the agent asks about next.

How a mission goes

  1. You give the mission and a few standing orders, such as “check with me before any email goes out”.
  2. The agent decides what to ask and has Flashpoint build the survey. It can rehearse it on AI personas first.
  3. Everyone seals a guess. The agent's, the personas' and yours. The agent cannot see yours.
  4. You approve the send. No email goes out without your yes. The code enforces that, not the agent.
  5. The envelope is opened against the real answers, and the agent says who was wrong, itself first.
  6. The agent decides the next move: a second round on what surprised everyone, or the answer.

Every paid step prints a receipt first: what it costs, and the decision it could change. The agent works inside a budget cap that the code holds.
