## Answer

**Recommendation.** Replace the current newsletter with a short monthly email led by club decisions and the reasoning behind them, plus key dates. Add a plain list of volunteering and career opportunities, and label sponsored offers as paid placements. Cut back member stories and photos, and don't send weekly. Send schedule changes separately on the same day.

**Evidence.** Round 1 (5 of 8 answered): club news and decisions averaged rank 2.0 with 3 first places, event dates 2.0, opportunities 3.0, and member stories came last at 5.4. Frequency was close: monthly 1.8, only when important 2.0, fortnightly 2.2, weekly 4.0. Email was the top channel (1.2, 4 first places). Round 2 (8 of 8 answered): a monthly decisions-and-dates email got 4 votes, an email only when something important happens got 2, fortnightly got 1, and a web page got 1. Trust ranking: decisions with reasoning 1.63, labelled offers 2.13, published votes and dissent 2.63, wider range of members 3.63. Open answers: committee decisions are made behind closed doors, offers may be paid placements, the same few members feature in stories, and one member wanted print.
